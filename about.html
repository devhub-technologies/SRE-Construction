About Us

SRE Construction, Bhavani

SRE Construction is a Bhavani based construction company led by Er. S.G. Selvaraj, D.C.E., L.B.S. We believe a home or a building is more than bricks and cement it is the result of a dream, careful planning and honest workmanship. Our motto, "Building Your Dreams", guides every project we take up.

We are not only builders, we are also planners and engineers. From the first computerised plan and cost estimate to valuation, building contract and the final handover, our team supports you at every stage so that you know what you are building, what it will cost and how it will be done.

Whether you are building a new house, constructing a commercial building, renovating an old property, planning interior works, or need an ETP tank built, SRE Construction brings quality materials, innovative work methods and transparent dealings to your project.


  • Planning, Estimate & Valuation by Qualified Engineers
  • Construction, Interior & Renovation Under One Roof
  • Clear Building Contracts with Transparent Costing
  • Quality Work, Innovative Methods & Customer Satisfaction
